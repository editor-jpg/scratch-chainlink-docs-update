Predict the Worlds

In Predict the Worlds, a $250,000 prize pool awaits those trading on the League of Legends World Championship.

In this activity, you’ll place predictions on LoL markets (both pre-match and in-match). Doing so will earn you Lootboxes at the end of every match, which you’ll open up after the Final to reveal your prizes.

Worlds Structure

LoL Worlds runs from October 15 to November 14.

19 top teams from around the globe will battle it out across three stages, with the anticipated dates as follows:

  • October 15–18 (Play-Ins): four teams will play Bo5, double elimination, with only one team advancing to the next round.
  • October 23–31 (Swiss Stage): 16 teams go in, with only eight progressing. Three wins to advance, and three losses to get eliminated.
  • November 3–14 (Knockouts): the remaining eight teams are whittled down to one winner across Quarterfinals, Semifinals and the Final.

How to Participate

Both on-site and API trades will count towards your Predict the Worlds performance. All you need to do to sign up is hit Join Now on the activity page.

During the match, you’ll want to trade relevant match markets. Pre-match markets affect your performance too, but in-match markets carry more weighting.

At the end of a match, you can check your summary card to see how many Lootboxes you’ve won, and optionally reveal their rarity.

Hint: By tapping Reveal, you’ll see the rarity of your Lootboxes. However, you won’t be able to open them until after the Final.

Lootboxes and Rarity

Lootboxes contain USDT, the amount of which is determined by their rarity. In order of ascending value, these are categorized as:

  • Common
  • Uncommon
  • Rare
  • Epic
  • Legendary

The USDT value of these remains a secret until they’re opened post-Final. Evidently, the rarer the box, the greater the prize.

Special Boxes

Regular Lootboxes aren’t all you’ll find, though. If you’re lucky, you’ll come across a Special Box — a box containing separate physical and digital prizes:

  • Steam Gift Cards ($50)
  • Riot Points ($100)
  • Razer Huntsman ($200)
  • Secretlab Worlds Edition Chair ($700)
  • Riftbound x T1 2025 Champion Signature Edition ($2,000)

Where physical rewards are won, Predict will instead provide the cash value in USDT.

Opening Lootboxes

Lootboxes will be opened on November 15. You don’t need to take any action to do so – this is an automatic process, with your USDT deposited directly to your Predict account.
